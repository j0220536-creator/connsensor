Our Story

CONNSENSOR is the international OEM brand of Zhongshan Aopuda Electric Appliance Co., Ltd., specializing in wire harness and temperature sensor solutions for home appliance manufacturers.

With years of manufacturing experience, we provide customized wiring assemblies, thermal protection sensors, and connector solutions for global appliance brands. Our products are widely used in air fryers, water heaters, refrigerators, coffee machines, washing machines, and other household appliances across the United States and Southeast Asia markets.

Backed by automated production, strict quality control, and flexible OEM/ODM support, we help customers achieve stable production, reliable performance, and fast project development. We believe reliable products build reliable partnerships.
